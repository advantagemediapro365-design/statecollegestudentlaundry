Student Laundry in State College

State College Student Laundry offers convenient laundry pickup and delivery for students in State College, including Penn State students living in eligible dorms and off-campus apartments. Spend less time sorting, washing, drying, and folding clothes, and more time on classes, work, activities, and rest. Our wash, dry, and fold service returns clean laundry ready to put away. Pickup arrangements and availability depend on your location, so contact us to confirm that your building and address are within our service area.

Pickup, wash, dry, fold, and delivery

Request a pickup through our scheduling form and share your State College address and preferred arrangements. We will follow up to confirm eligibility, pickup details, and availability before your service is scheduled. After collection, laundry is washed, dried, and neatly folded, then returned to the agreed location. This simple process is designed for student routines, whether you live near campus or in an eligible apartment farther away. Visit How It Works for the steps, or check service-area eligibility before requesting a pickup.

Laundry help for students and parents

Shared laundry rooms, busy class schedules, and carrying laundry across campus can make this chore hard to fit into the week. Student laundry pickup gives you another option when you need clean clothes without making a laundromat trip. Parents can also help a student arrange service by sharing the student's location and contacting our team to confirm pickup arrangements. Learn about student laundry plans or visit the page for parents for details.

Plans, pricing, and service details

Choose from available per-pound service options and ask us which plan fits your laundry routine. We will confirm current pricing, plan details, and service availability directly, so you know what to expect before scheduling.
