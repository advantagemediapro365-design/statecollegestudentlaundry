Student Laundry in State College

State College Student Laundry offers convenient laundry pickup and delivery for students in State College, including Penn State students living in eligible dorms and off-campus apartments. Spend less time sorting, washing, drying, and folding clothes, and more time on classes, work, activities, and rest. Our wash, dry, and fold service returns clean laundry ready to put away. Pickup arrangements and availability depend on your location, so contact us to confirm that your building and address are within our service area.

Pickup, wash, dry, fold, and delivery

Request a pickup through our scheduling form and share your State College address and preferred arrangements. We will follow up to confirm eligibility, pickup details, and availability before your service is scheduled. After collection, laundry is washed, dried, and neatly folded, then returned to the agreed location. This simple process is designed for student routines, whether you live near campus or in an eligible apartment farther away. Laundry pickup designed around student schedules

A full week of lectures, labs, work shifts, practices, and study time can make it difficult to find a free block for laundry. A pickup and delivery service can help by moving the washing and folding off your to-do list. Instead of planning a trip around shared machines or a laundromat, you can ask about an available pickup, then use the time for studying, meals, rest, or other parts of campus life. The service is intended for everyday student laundry and is available only when your specific location and pickup arrangements can be confirmed.

What to include when requesting a pickup

A clear request makes it easier to check eligibility and follow up with useful information. Include your name and preferred way to be contacted, your State College address or building, whether you live in a dorm or an apartment, and the days or times that generally fit your schedule. If your building has a designated entrance or a particular handoff procedure, mention that too. You can also ask questions about available plans, current pricing, laundry preparation, and how the return delivery is arranged. We will review the details and confirm what is available before treating a pickup as scheduled.

Choosing a laundry plan

Students have different laundry needs. One person may want help occasionally during exams or a busy stretch of the semester, while another may prefer to ask about a recurring schedule. Review the available options on the services and pricing page and consider how often you typically need a wash, dry, and fold service. Ask about the current per-pound rates, any minimums or plan conditions, payment details, and how scheduling works before deciding. Plan availability and terms should be confirmed directly; the right choice depends on your routine and the service options currently offered.
